Version:  0.2.3
Date:     Oct. 11th, 2021

    - PLAT-90094: [RMB_B0_FP7r2] - type-c ports do not work after shutdown and
                  remove ac from system for 15 seconds with a battery connected to the system
    - [RMB Lilac] Fix the VCI USB C10 not work issue.
    - [RMB Lilac] Fix the PS8830/PS8828 error in Bios option re-timer type.
    - PLAT-92647: [RMB] Can not boot after enabling DC only mode in BIOS. F/R:11/30
    - Add ACPI interface for DCProchotL tuning
        ECRAMxB4 [1:0] - Prochot Debounce (0/1/2/3 stand for 7us/100us/500us/1ms)
        ECRAMxB4 [4:2] - Prochot Duration (0~7 stand for 10ms/20ms/15ms/5ms/1ms/500us/100us/0s)
        ECRAMxB5 [5:0] - 0    = tie the DCProchotL to real batter level
                         0x3F = (default) 5A in DC only case or 12A in Fake DC case
                         1~0x3E = Specific the high byte of DCProchotL threshold.
                                  e.g. 0x28 stand for 10240mA; 0x14 stands for 5120mA
        Other fields of ECRAMxB4 and ECRAMxB5 are reserved.
